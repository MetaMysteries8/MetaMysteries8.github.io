# VoiceEnable — in brief

A voice-first AI agent in your browser, powered by `gpt-realtime-2`.

**Talk to it.** Have a real spoken conversation in realtime, or use Push2Talk (transcription + a normal model) for a lighter, cheaper setup. You can also just type.

**Ask it to make things.** Images, videos, music, speech — it routes to the right model and saves results to your local gallery. It can edit images, turn images into video, and build runnable HTML apps with a coding model.

**Watch it build.** An adaptive canvas renders charts, tables, checklists, code, and even custom interactive widgets the coder generates on the fly.

**It looks things up.** Built-in web search gives current answers with sources.

**It remembers you.** Persistent long-term memory carries your name, preferences, and projects across sessions.

**It manages your media.** Everything generated lives in a local gallery the agent can also clean up for you.

**Experimental: Gibberlink.** Two agents can talk to each other over sound (data-as-audio tones) instead of speech.

**You're in control.** Swap any model, bring your own API key (your Pollen, no bundled secrets), pick themes, layouts, personalities, and sound.

No installs, no backend — a single static page.
