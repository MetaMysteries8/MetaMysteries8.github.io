MAINVOICE SINGING EDITOR

Make the voice sing

Draw notes onto the piano roll, give each note a lyric or ARPAbet override, and MainVoice turns the phonemes into sung syllables while preserving their one-time attacks and releases.

EXPERIMENTAL · LOCAL
Recorded base note matters: set this to the pitch you used when holding the voiced phonemes. Unvoiced sounds such as S/SH/F/P/T/K stay noise-like instead of being forced onto a musical note.

Long notes do not loop the full WAV: MainVoice plays the attack once, stretches only a stable sustain region, then plays the release once. Diphthong glides such as AY/OW are kept toward the note ending instead of repeating.
Tip: one syllable per note sounds best. Multi-syllable words still work, but are squeezed inside one note.
