TIMING TRICKS

Make it feel conversational

Pause after: positive values leave silence before the next line.
Interrupt: use a negative pause, like -300 ms, so the next speaker starts early.
Pan: slightly separate speakers in stereo without making them sound hard-left/right.
Expression: punctuation still matters — ?, !, commas, and ellipses shape the delivery.
